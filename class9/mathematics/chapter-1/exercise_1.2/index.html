What are applications of real numbers? Exercise 1.3, "Applications of Real Numbers," focuses on solving practical, real-life word problems using arithmetic operations and algebraic formulas. This exercise covers diverse real-world scenarios where mathematics is applied daily.

How do we solve banking and financial problems? You will learn banking calculations including managing balances, deposits, withdrawals, and cheque processing. Example problems involve calculating final account balance after series of transactions.

How do we calculate fuel efficiency and unit costs? You will learn to determine average fuel efficiency in km/liter, calculate unit costs for materials, and solve distance-speed-time problems using the relation $Distance = Speed \times Time$.

How do we apply geometric formulas? You will use geometric formulas such as the perimeter of a rectangle ($P = 2(l + w)$) to solve for unknown dimensions like width when perimeter and length are given.

How do we handle fractions and ratios? A significant part of the exercise involves handling fractions and ratios in business contexts:

  • Calculating capital and debt from total assets
  • Distributing a penalty among shareholders based on their investment ratios using the sum of ratios method
  • Partitioning total amounts into specific components

How do we convert between temperature scales? You will master temperature conversions using standard mathematical formulas:

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: $F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32$
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: $C = \frac{5}{9}(F - 32)$
  • Celsius to Kelvin: $K = C + 273.15$
  • Kelvin to Celsius: $C = K - 273.15$
  • Example: $-89.2°C = 183.8 K$

What is the average fuel efficiency formula? Fuel efficiency = Total distance traveled / Total fuel consumed (km/liter). Pay attention to decimal placement (e.g., 17.5 km/l vs 1.75 km/l).
